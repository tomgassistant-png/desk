Attacks and Incidents

First Sabbath of the olive harvest: a youth from Givat Tidhar is struck on the head

On 10 October hundreds of Arabs from Deir Abu Mash'al, with foreign and far-left activists, some of them masked and carrying clubs, came up towards Givat Tidhar in Wadi Zarqa, western Binyamin, under the pretext of picking olives. Residents held back the crowd until the troops arrived; a youth from the hilltop was struck on the head and taken to Assaf Harofeh hospital, conscious. The Palestinian Red Crescent said nine Palestinians were injured at Deir Abu Mash'al, and four masked foreign activists taken to the Modi'in Illit police station elsewhere were later released without explanation.

Attacks and Incidents

Twenty-three attacks on Israelis in two days, most under cover of the harvest

The volunteer rescue network Hatzalah Judea and Samaria Without Borders listed 23 attacks on Israelis in Judea and Samaria on 9 and 10 October, which it said had largely gone unreported in the national media. They included stone-throwing near Or Yedidya that lightly injured a security officer, a vehicle set on fire by Arabs from Talfit, shepherds attacked or beaten near Ateret, Rachelim, al-Mughayyir and Osarin, and an attempt to seize a resident's weapon at the Kela David farm near Neve Tzuf. The booby-trapped PLO flag found on 9 October on Mount Hazor was destroyed in a controlled explosion on Saturday.

Hatzalah Judea and Samaria Without Borders · 10 October 2026 · Source

IDF and Government Decisions

Binyamin Brigade ends week-long operation; house of Netanel Shukron's murderer destroyed

The IDF Binyamin Brigade and the Border Police of Judea and Samaria completed a week-long operation in five villages, searching more than 600 locations, seizing weapons and incitement material, and arresting wanted men, among them suspects in the transfer of terror funds. The forces destroyed the house of the Hamas operative from Biddu who murdered Netanel Shukron at the Ein Ria spring near Neve Tzuf before Yom Kippur. During the same week dozens of people who had expressed support for the massacre of 7 October were arrested across Judea and Samaria.

Olam Katan · 10 October 2026 · Source

Har Bracha widow asks the army to destroy the monument to her husband's murderer

Lea Ziv-Parag, the widow of Yaakov Parag, who was murdered in December 1988 while guarding the lands of Har Bracha, has written to the head of Central Command, Major General Avi Bluth, asking the army to remove a monument to his murderer, Hamdan al-Najjar, which the family says stands near the place of the murder. The request follows the army's current campaign to remove monuments to terrorists in Judea and Samaria. MK Zvi Sukkot, chairman of the Knesset subcommittee on Judea and Samaria, joined the request and asked the IDF to act urgently.

Attacks and Incidents

Attacks on Israelis under cover of the olive harvest in Samaria and Binyamin

Olam Katan reported that on the morning of 9 October Arabs from Zeita waited in an olive grove in the Gush Kana area, between Yitzhar and Emanuel, and stoned the car of Israeli hikers; the windscreen was damaged and no one was hurt. Settlement and Land

Hundreds walk the Wallerstein Road and call for it to be reopened to Israelis

Hityashvut Achshav reported that on the morning of 9 October hundreds of hikers and residents, organised by the Chargavim group, walked from Beit El to Dolev along the Wallerstein Road, which has been closed to Israeli traffic for about 26 years, and held what the report called the first prayer on the road in 30 years. They called for full security and civil control of the road and for it to be reopened, which would cut the journey from Beit El to western Binyamin from about an hour to ten minutes. Settlement and Land

A further seizure order in Area A inside the city of Jenin

WAFA's Hebrew service reported on 6 October, citing the Palestinian Authority's Colonization and Wall Resistance Commission, that the IDF has issued seizure order No. 33/26 for 741 square metres in the al-Jabriyat neighbourhood of Jenin, in Area A. The order was signed on 5 October as immediate and urgent on grounds of military need, is valid for 60 days, and covers a narrow strip along a road route. It adjoins and partly overlaps an earlier order of 7 May 2026 for 7.247 dunams and extends the seized corridor eastward.

Palestinian Authority Forces and the Threat

Authority security men sent to the olive harvest in civilian clothes

Hakol Hayehudi reported that the Palestinian Authority regularly sends members of its security forces, often in civilian clothes, to olive harvests in Area C and next to Jewish communities, under the name of “protection from settlers”; the director-general of the Palestinian police, Major-General Alam al-Saqa, has ordered higher readiness for the season. The practice goes back to a decision of Prime Minister Salam Fayyad in 2011 and to a later framework called “harvest in the hot zones”, which sends officers and intelligence men to points of friction. The report followed the death at Burqa of Ali al-Bazari, an officer of the National Security Forces, in a clash with the IDF after a Jewish shepherd was attacked.

The Binyamin Regional Council has begun to give body cameras to farmers after a sharp rise in incidents at the start of the olive harvest. Settlement and Land

Seventeen settlements approved in the Jenin district since early 2025

According to figures from Peace Now cited by +972 Magazine, the government has approved 17 settlements in the Jenin district since the start of 2025, among them four evacuated in 2005; Ganim and Kadim have now been re-established, and Defence Minister Katz spoke at the ceremony for Ganim on 13 August. The army has set up six new camps across the district, one of them inside the city of Jenin, and another is planned. In July the commander of Central Command, Major General Avi Bluth, signed at least 15 orders taking about 200 dunams in Areas A and C for a road between the new settlements of Emek Dotan and Noa; Peace Now says this is the first use of such orders in Area A for the needs of settlement since the Oslo Accords.

Palestinian Authority Forces and the Threat

Katz: what was done in Gaza and Lebanon will come to Judea and Samaria

Defence Minister Israel Katz said this week that the IDF is preparing as never before for a “turning of the barrels”, in which the forces of the Palestinian Authority would turn their weapons on Israelis. “If anyone dares to turn weapons against us, what we did in Gaza and Lebanon will come to Judea and Samaria,” he said, adding that Israel would answer with an all-out campaign. Dr. Edy Cohen told Channel 14 that the threat is “not science fiction”, recalled that such forces turned on their hosts in Jordan, Lebanon and Kuwait, and criticised the security cabinet for not yet deciding on Minister Ben Gvir’s call to dismantle the Authority.
