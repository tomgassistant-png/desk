Settlement and Land

NewWallerstein Road: the IDF has begun preparations to reopen Route 463

In an exclusive interview with Olam Katan, published on 8 October, Israel Ganz, head of the Binyamin Regional Council and of the Yesha Council, said that Wallerstein Road (Route 463) is joining the Ma'ozim axis plan and that the IDF has begun work toward its official opening to Israeli traffic. The road runs from Nili Junction in the west to Road 466 near Al-Bireh in the east; it was closed to Israelis during the Second Intifada, and the drive between the Talmonim bloc and the Beit El area lengthened from about a quarter of an hour to about an hour. Mr. Ganz said that the army must shift bases and equipment, that the work is already budgeted, that legal preparations are under way, and that the process is a matter of several months; the principal decision has been taken, but the road is not yet open for routine Israeli travel.

Olam Katan · 8 October 2026 · Read the source

IDF and Government Decisions

NewNorthern Samaria: Islamic Jihad cell planning an attack is arrested

Maariv reported on the morning of 13 October that IDF forces of the Manashe Brigade, directed by the Shin Bet, completed two arrests during the week in the villages of Qabatiya and Arraba, in which four operatives of Islamic Jihad who were planning an attack in northern Samaria were detained. In a separate operation in the Beit Rima area of the Binyamin Brigade, fighters of the Duvdevan unit arrested a wanted man suspected of trafficking in weapons. Channel 14, citing the IDF Spokesperson, reported that the detainees were transferred for further interrogation and that there were no casualties among Israeli forces.

Maariv · 13 October 2026 · Source

NewSamaria security coordinators block Tapuah Junction over the Shin Bet arrests

Hakol Hayehudi reported on 13 October that military security coordinators from Samaria communities blocked Tapuah Junction with their security vehicles in protest at the detention of two Samaria security men arrested by the Shin Bet at the start of the week, under a gag order and initially barred from meeting their lawyers. The slogan of the protest was "A ravshatz is not a terrorist." Adv. Nati Rom of Honenu, who met the detainees after the court accepted the appeal against the meeting ban, said they were being held in harsh conditions in a Shin Bet facility, in a small crowded cell without sunlight and with their eyes covered.

IDF and Government Decisions

Katz answers the High Court: the IDF will remain in the Jenin, Tulkarm and Nur Shams camps

Defence Minister Israel Katz stated on 12 October, after the High Court asked for a timetable for the return of residents, that the IDF will stay in the three northern Samaria camps with a permanent hold. Settlement and Land

Harvest orders: more land under IDF coordination, less closed to Israelis

Peace Now reported that the head of Central Command has signed the harvest orders for 2026: Palestinians may enter about 88,300 dunams of olive groves only with prior coordination with the IDF, about 30 percent more than the 68,800 dunams of 2025 (a dunam is 1,000 square metres). The area closed to Israelis during the harvest has fallen from about 10,700 dunams in 2025 to 6,890 dunams this year. According to Peace Now, the coordination zones are drawn to protect new farms and hilltop communities and cover close to 20 percent of the olive groves in Judea and Samaria.
