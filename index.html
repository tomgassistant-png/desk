Attacks and Incidents

NewAbu Falah: what the evidence shows about the attack on the harvest and on a Washington Post photographer

A review of the reports, the statements and five clips, examined frame by frame, establishes the following about the morning of 16 October. Several dozen masked men came down from a new hilltop outpost in Area B above the groves of Khirbet Abu Falah. They set fires and slung stones at the harvesters, and village youths threw stones back. Reuters, AP and the Post say so, the raw video shows it, and the IDF, as quoted by N12, said that dozens of rioters came down from an illegal outpost. Heidi Levine, 64, a Post photographer in a vest marked PRESS, was knocked down and struck by four to six hooded men, not by one man. Her cameras, phone and passport were taken and have not been reported recovered. The Post reports a broken rib, four broken vertebrae and a finger torn to the bone. The JSR and Binyamin Council clip, which says an Israeli teenager helped her up, is edited. It joins a later shout to a zoomed shot in which she stays seated among the hooded men. It leaves out the earlier part of the same raw video, in which men stand over a person on the ground. What it shows fits her account that she was pulled up and knocked down again. The Palestinian who was shot, council member Abduljaber Hassan, 54, was hit in the thigh by soldiers, not by a settler. The IDF says he ran at troops with a sharp object, and the village denies it. Who threw the first stone, whether fireworks were fired, and who the attackers were remain unknown, and no arrests have been made. Full timeline and footage review in the backgrounder.

Washington Post; CNN; AP; Reuters; N12; Israel Hayom; Arutz 7; JSR; X footage · 16 October 2026 · Read the source

NewJSR: Palestinian Authority police officers take part in the olive harvest in Salfit

The Judea and Samaria Situation Room (JSR) reported on 16 October that officers of the Palestinian Authority police took part in the olive harvest in the town of Salfit in Samaria, and that the PA police published photos of their participation on its official Facebook page. Hatzalah Judea and Samaria: 63 attacks in the first 13 days of October

Hatzalah Judea and Samaria Without Borders counted 63 reported Palestinian terror incidents from 1 to 13 October, more than twice the 29 it recorded in all of September, according to a TPS report carried on 15 October.
