NewHebron operation: forces find an underground dig about 15 meters deep next to the home of one of the Judah junction attackers

During the counterterror operation in Hebron, forces on Sunday found an underground dig next to the home of one of the terrorists behind Friday's Judah junction attack, the IDF Spokesperson said, as reported by Srugim. NewJSR answers a PA TV claim that a settler poisoned his own flock: it says the "poison" was charcoal powder used to treat poisoned animals

Awda TV, the PA's official channel, claimed that a "settler" near al-Mughayyir was filmed poisoning his own flock in order to blame the village. JSR rejected the claim in its own account: it says Jewish farmers carry charcoal powder as a veterinary treatment for animals that have been poisoned, and that this is what was filmed (JSR Hebrew; JSR English). JSR also says that since January 2026 it has recorded 18 poisoning incidents against Jewish shepherds, with more than 23 goats and sheep killed (JSR English). NewMinisters, MKs and Judea and Samaria council heads sign a demand to cancel Oslo as a condition for the next government

A day before the election, ministers, Knesset members, Judea and Samaria council heads and public figures signed a letter asking that cancelling the Oslo Accords be a basic condition in coalition talks, Olam Katan reports. Attacks and Incidents

New"We were a step from disaster": after three attacks in three days, Yitzhar asks the army to clear buildings and trees along its access road

The Yitzhar secretariat sent an urgent letter to the Samaria Brigade commander, Col. David Cohen, after stones were thrown on Sunday night at families driving home on the community's access road, Arutz 7 reports. According to the secretariat, the attackers came from the village of Burin and struck a few dozen meters from an army post, using darkness and nearby buildings and orchards for cover. It says this followed an attack on Friday on hikers on an ATV trip and stones thrown at a bus on Saturday night. The community asks the army to demolish buildings and uproot trees next to the road, including a house at the junction and another across the road, to cancel all olive-harvest permits in the area, and to carry out demolition orders on illegal building in Area C outside Burin. Copies went to Defense Minister Israel Katz, Minister Bezalel Smotrich, Central Command chief Avi Bluth and Samaria Regional Council head Yossi Dagan. "We were a step from disaster," the secretariat said. Also reported by Yotam Deshe on X.

Palestinian Authority Forces and the Threat

HaKol HaYehudi maps the PA's National Security battalions: about 8,000 men, and a Salfit base 340 meters from Ariel's fence

The settler news site HaKol HaYehudi published a map of the Palestinian Authority's National Security Forces, dated 22 October and promoted on 25 October by Meir Ettinger. It says the force has 10 battalions, 9 tied to regions plus a mobile unit called Battalion 101, with about 8,000 men in all, plus more than 60 police points whose stations hold 15 to 40 armed officers each. Training is at Camp Nuweimeh near Jericho, and most battalion commanders trained in Algeria. A new commander took over in 2026, Maj. Gen. al-Abd Ibrahim Abd al-Salam Khalil, replacing Nidal Abu Dukhan. The site says the Salfit base sits about 340 meters from the fence of Ariel, under district commander Col. Ahmad al-Baz, and that the Jenin battalion sits at the Horsh Saada camp, on the ruins of an abandoned Nahal army outpost. These figures are the site's own and have not been confirmed elsewhere.
