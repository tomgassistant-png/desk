Who in the UFO and UAP world is tied to whom, and through which organizations. Every link is numbered and listed with its source below.

Solid lines were checked against a specific source other than Wikipedia; dashed lines are reported but unconfirmed. Node size grows with the number of links. Updated Oct 30, 2026 (Jerusalem time).

Key findings

1. Hal Puthoff is the main broker. He is the only person who links the 1970s CIA-funded SRI remote viewing work (with Russell Targ and Kit Green), EarthTech and Eric Davis, Bigelow's AAWSAP study, and To The Stars Academy. Remove him and the old psychic-research circle and the 2017 disclosure group barely touch.

2. Robert Bigelow's organizations form the densest cluster, and it vouches for itself. NIDS, Skinwalker Ranch and BAASS/AAWSAP tie together Bigelow, Colm Kelleher, James Lacatski, George Knapp, Davis, Puthoff and Green, who co-write each other's books. Much apparent confirmation from several sources traces back to this one group.

3. Garry Nolan and Jacques Vallee bridge the academic wing to the insiders, and the Sol Foundation is a second hub. Its advisory board joins Vallee, Diana Walsh Pasulka, Jeffrey Kripal, Davis, Avi Loeb and Beatriz Villarroel, with Tim Gallaudet as senior adviser. Together with the Galileo Project's 2021 recruitment of Christopher Mellon and Luis Elizondo, that pulls even the data-first astronomers into the same circle.

Who's who

J. Allen Hynek Old guard

Astronomer, Air Force Project Blue Book adviser, coined 'close encounters.' Died 1986. Connections: Jacques Vallee (co-authors); Invisible College (colleagues / co-founded); John Keel (Playboy assignment).

Jacques Vallee Old guard

Computer scientist and author; control-system and folklore theories; Hynek protege. Connections: J. John Mack Old guard

Harvard psychiatrist who studied abduction experiencers. Died 2004. Connections: Robert Bigelow (funded); Jeffrey Kripal (interviewed / promoted).

John Keel Old guard

Journalist (1930–2009), author of UFOs: Operation Trojan Horse (1970) and The Mothman Prophecies (1975). Argued that "ultraterrestrials" native to Earth disguise themselves as whatever each era expects. See the Keel backgrounder. Not on the map: parapsychologist George Hansen (friend; built his trickster theory on Keel and Vallee) and Ivan Sanderson (Keel reportedly edited for his SITU group, unconfirmed). Connections: Jacques Vallee (corresponded / met); J. Allen Hynek (Playboy assignment); Gray Barker (friends / rival books); Jeffrey Kripal (wrote about him); Diana Walsh Pasulka (discussed him).

Organizations

Invisible College

Informal circle of scientists Hynek and Vallee gathered in the 1960s and 70s to study UFOs quietly. Linked to: J. Allen Hynek (colleagues / co-founded); Jacques Vallee (colleagues / co-founded).

SRI / remote viewing

CIA-funded psychic research at Stanford Research Institute, 1972 onward; later Stargate. Linked to: Jacques Vallee (worked together); Russell Targ (worked together); Hal Puthoff (worked together); Kit Green (worked together).

NIDS

National Institute for Discovery Science, Bigelow-funded research group, 1995 to 2004. Linked to: Robert Bigelow (funded); Jacques Vallee (colleagues / co-founded); Colm Kelleher (worked together); Kit Green (colleagues / co-founded).

Skinwalker Ranch

Utah ranch Bigelow bought in 1996; studied by NIDS and BAASS. Private. Linked to: Robert Bigelow (funded); Colm Kelleher (worked together); James Lacatski (worked together).

BAASS / AAWSAP

Bigelow contractor that ran the Pentagon-funded AAWSAP study, 2008 to 2010. Linked to: Kit Green (worked together); Robert Bigelow (funded); James Lacatski (worked together); Colm Kelleher (worked together); Hal Puthoff (worked together); Eric Davis (worked together); AATIP (worked together).

AATIP

Pentagon UAP effort that Elizondo says he ran; revealed by the New York Times in 2017. Linked to: Luis Elizondo (worked together); BAASS / AAWSAP (worked together); Jay Stratton (worked together).

To The Stars Academy

Company DeLonge launched in 2017 with Puthoff, Elizondo, Mellon and others. Linked to: Tom DeLonge (colleagues / co-founded); Hal Puthoff (colleagues / co-founded); Luis Elizondo (colleagues / co-founded); Christopher Mellon (colleagues / co-founded).

Sol Foundation

UAP think tank founded in 2022 by Nolan and Skafish. Linked to: Garry Nolan (colleagues / co-founded); Peter Skafish (colleagues / co-founded); Diana Walsh Pasulka (colleagues / co-founded); Jacques Vallee (colleagues / co-founded); Jeffrey Kripal (colleagues / co-founded); Eric Davis (colleagues / co-founded); Avi Loeb (colleagues / co-founded); Beatriz Villarroel (colleagues / co-founded); Tim Gallaudet (colleagues / co-founded); Karl Nell (colleagues / co-founded).

Galileo Project

Harvard-based effort building telescopes to watch for anomalous objects. Linked to: Luis Elizondo (colleagues / co-founded); Avi Loeb (colleagues / co-founded); Christopher Mellon (colleagues / co-founded).
