27 October 2026

Andreessen publishes a new essay on AI agents and the "self-driving company"

On 26 October 2026, Marc Andreessen published an essay titled "Let Chaos Reign, and Then Rein In Chaos" (subtitle "Replit and the dawn of the self-driving company") on the a16z newsletter site, and posted it as an X article from @pmarca at 16:08 UTC (18:08 Israel time). It was written for the launch of Replit for Business, a product of the a16z portfolio company Replit. He argues that important workplace technologies such as the personal computer, the spreadsheet and email first arrived in a disorderly way, and that companies then had to build controlled settings that kept employee initiative alive. He says AI agents are following the same path, and that companies will become "self-driving" when people set goals and agents carry out the work. It is his first written essay found since "Investing in Cognition" on 9 September 2026, and his first original X post found since 18 October. His X posting is still far below his pre-July level: his total post count rose by 4 in the day to 27 October, and he made 47 posts in the seven days to 27 October (about 7 a day), compared with about 25 a day before July.

23 October 2026

Andreessen appears on camera in a new a16z Show episode announcing a16z Frontier

On 22 October 2026, a16z published a one-hour video podcast, "The Frontier Ideas a16z Wants to Fund Now," in which Marc Andreessen, Ben Horowitz and Chris Dixon, with host Erik Torenberg, announce that the a16z crypto fund is becoming a16z Frontier. A transcript service counts about 17 minutes of speaking time for Andreessen. In a clip posted by a16z, he says: "The AI-native early stage startups have just a completely different view on what they can do with AI than the typical incumbents in almost any industry." The episode also covers AI agents, open source AI, and what comes after the iPhone. It is the first new podcast or video appearance found since the CLARITY Act episode in early August. On X he still made no original posts: his posts from 22 to 23 October were all reposts, including the a16z Frontier announcement. His total X post count rose by 5 in the day to 23 October, about 20 percent of his pre-July rate of about 25 a day.

19 October 2026

Andreessen posts an original film comment on X: "At long last, movies are back"

On 18 October 2026 at 8:01 a.m. Israel time, Marc Andreessen wrote on X: "YOU CAN SEE EVERYTHING, PRIMETIME, and DIGGER make 2026 the best year for film since 2015 (SICARIO, EX MACHINA, and MAD MAX: FURY ROAD). At long last, movies are back." It is his first original post found since his 4 October recommendation of DIGGER, and X showed about 528,000 views by the morning of 19 October.
