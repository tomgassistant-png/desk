15 October 2026 · today

NewCursor opens an Origin App Marketplace with more than 20 partner apps

On 13 October Cursor's changelog announced an Origin App Marketplace with more than 20 apps for deployments, CI, code review, security, monitoring and project management. Named partners include Cloudflare, Netlify, Render, Spacelift and Vercel for deployments; Buildkite, CircleCI and others for CI; CodeRabbit, Greptile and Tenki for code review; Artemis Security, Semgrep, Socket Security and Upwind for security; Sentry for monitoring; and Linear for project management. Public submissions are open: teams build against the Origin API, submit a slug, tags and a demo video, and Cursor reviews before listing. There is nothing new on the official Grok Bot changelog since version 0.70.2 (11 October), already reported, and nothing material on SpaceX beyond the Flight 15 catch claim already covered on 12 October (Cursor changelog).

14 October 2026

Grok Bot 0.70.2: Bots get their own email addresses and can search X

Version 0.70.2, released on 11 October, lets a Bot claim its own email address and receive mail there. Any email it sends from that address waits for the user's approval. Bots can now also search X and read posts without a connected X account, and some connectors, such as Figma, Notion and Canva, can appear as interactive cards in the chat. The release also lets Bots pay with X Money or Stripe Link. The official changelog is the source. The Botski Blog, an unofficial site, also reports the X search and the email feature, but it does not confirm the version number or the payment feature, which are therefore unconfirmed by a second source.

Grok Bot 0.68: slide decks, formatted email and Gmail send-as addresses

Version 0.68.0 (5 October) lets email drafts go out from a verified Gmail send-as address and adds five languages: Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Swahili and Hausa. Version 0.68.1 (7 October) lets Bots build slide decks as PowerPoint files or Google Slides, send formatted emails, and search the Gmail Spam folder. It also enlarges the Bot's computer screen to 1920 × 1200. The official changelog, the Grok Bot Wiki and the Botski Blog all report these changes.

Grok Bot 0.66–0.67: the primary Bot offers to take on work; Marketplace becomes Connect Apps

Version 0.66.0 (2 October) lets the user's primary Bot, the one marked with a star, notice work it could take on and offer to do it. A second release on the same day renamed the Marketplace to Connect Apps and moved Bot templates to the web. The official changelog, the Grok Bot Wiki and the Botski Blog all report these changes.

12 October 2026

Musk says Starship Flight 15 will try to catch the upper stage

Speaking by video at the X Takeover fan event in Texas on Saturday, Musk said that Starship Flight 15 will try to catch the upper stage with the launch tower, which would be the first full recovery of an orbital rocket, and that he hopes it will fly in four to six weeks, "a little bit before Thanksgiving." These are his claims. SpaceX has not published a mission plan, and the FAA, which licenses every launch and reentry, has not changed the license. NASASpaceflight notes that the current license covers only the Flight 14 reentry, and that changing the mission profile to a ship catch could take time to approve. Its unofficial schedule, taken from FAA planning slides, puts Flight 15 (Ship 42 and Booster 22, from Pad 2 at Starbase) no earlier than 8 November. Ship 42 has had its six engines removed and has gone back for a third round of pressure testing, perhaps to fix the vacuum-engine failures seen on Flights 12 and 14 (NASASpaceflight). There is nothing material on xAI or Cursor.
