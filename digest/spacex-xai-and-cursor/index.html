21 October 2026

SpaceX's third quarter: revenue up 161 percent to $14.6 billion and a $4.4 billion profit, driven by AI; Cursor added $608 million

SpaceX reported third-quarter revenue of $14.575 billion on 20 October, up 161 percent from $5.576 billion a year earlier and from $7.814 billion in the second quarter, well above the LSEG analyst estimate of $12.72 billion (SEC filing; Reuters). Net income was $4.404 billion, against a loss of $1.054 billion a year earlier; operating income was $2.791 billion and adjusted EBITDA $8.809 billion. Diluted GAAP earnings were 35 cents a share; Reuters, using adjusted figures, reports 45 cents against an expected 13 cents. By segment: AI (the 'SI' segment, which includes xAI and Cursor) brought in $7.560 billion, up 195 percent from the second quarter and 830 percent from a year earlier, with operating income of $431 million after $1.2 billion of share-based pay tied to the Cursor purchase; Connectivity (Starlink) $5.248 billion; Space $1.767 billion. Capital spending was $19.593 billion, of which $16.444 billion went to AI. Starlink has 13.6 million consumer subscribers paying an average of $69 a month, and the company has 1.7 gigawatts of nameplate computing capacity as Colossus II is built. The Cursor deal closed on 14 August at an implied equity value of $60 billion (GAAP consideration $42.5 billion), and Cursor contributed $608 million of revenue through 30 September. The release also names Starship Flight 14 as the first revenue-earning orbital flight (26 Starlink V3 satellites), a planned Starbase in Louisiana with a first launch targeted for 2029, and an 800 MHz spectrum deal signed on 8 October, and it mentions Grok Bot. Yahoo Finance reports a backlog of $63 billion, $42 billion of it expected within a year.

Grok Bot changelog adds version 0.71.2: tagged email addresses, prefilled forms, 250 MB attachments and up to 200 plugin skills

The official Grok Bot changelog now lists version 0.71.2, dated 14 October, as the latest release; earlier editions of this digest found only version 0.70.2 of 11 October. New: Bots can send from tagged forms of their email address (such as name+shop@) with routines that run only for that tag; forms a Bot asks you to fill can arrive with known fields filled in; team admins can stop members' Bots from messaging or handing work to other Bots; and a Bot whose computer runs low on disk names the largest files and deletes them only with your agreement. Improved: attachments of up to 250 MB and videos of up to 1 GB, up to 20 attachments per message instead of 6, up to 200 skills from installed plugins instead of 24, dropped voice calls that reconnect and continue, notifications on the computer in use rather than the phone, and batch actions on Outlook mail. The release also lists dozens of fixes, including longer connector time limits (two minutes instead of one) and fewer X rate-limit errors. This is the company's own changelog; the release date is the one it gives and could not be checked elsewhere. Cursor's changelog and SpaceXSI's news page show nothing new since the last edition.

SpaceXSI releases Grok Voice 3.0; tops Artificial Analysis speech-to-speech ranking

Musk says Starship Flight 15 will try to catch the upper stage

Speaking by video at the X Takeover fan event in Texas on Saturday, Musk said that Starship Flight 15 will try to catch the upper stage with the launch tower, which would be the first full recovery of an orbital rocket, and that he hopes it will fly in four to six weeks, "a little bit before Thanksgiving." These are his claims. SpaceX has not published a mission plan, and the FAA, which licenses every launch and reentry, has not changed the license. Its unofficial schedule, taken from FAA planning slides, puts Flight 15 (Ship 42 and Booster 22, from Pad 2 at Starbase) no earlier than 8 November. Ship 42 has had its six engines removed and has gone back for a third round of pressure testing, perhaps to fix the vacuum-engine failures seen on Flights 12 and 14 (NASASpaceflight).
