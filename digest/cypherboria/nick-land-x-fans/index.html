0:03:11 Cyphers team: gematria and AI

Chapter title: Cyphers Team, Gematria & AI. Summary written from the captions; Eric asks, Land answers (the chapter title and the host's introduction identify Eric as the questioner).

Eric asks how Land works in this field: does he look for resonance from outside, or for something that someone encoded? Land says there are several threads. Sometimes a term enters public attention and he checks its value. There is also a synthetic side, where he builds a cabalistic product on purpose. He calls the synthetic side problematic if it is treated as epistemology, but says the whole field has to be robust enough to cope. Often he does not know what he is doing until some steps later.

Land uses his recent move toward a Based Atlantean cipher as the example. He starts from Crowley's two-letter key, AL. AL has the value 31 in Hebrew and Greek, and also in Alphanumeric Qabbala (AQ). He says he was "a complete chauvinist" about AQ for a long time and now takes other ciphers seriously, among them Synx, Satanic gematria and English Standard (ES). If AQ is subtracted from ES, some words give a zero result. He describes AQ and ES as a modern and an ancient decimal system with different properties.

For Land, a single number often matters more than a match between ciphers. His example is 210, which is "countdown" in AQ. Eric points out that 210 and related dates reduce to the same digits, and asks whether people intend such matches. Land answers that intentional behavior in this space is very small as a share of what happens.

On why English: Land says English is the task he was handed. He lacks the competence to do Chinese number systems. He recommends the King James Bible material of Brandon Peterson (the captions spell the name "Brendan Peterson") and says Peterson explains "why English". He is open to birth-date numerology but says life is short, and cites John Michael Greer's view that divination works if done seriously. Eric then asks about sovereign AI. Land says he is in "trust the plan" mode, that simple determinism cannot be right, but that providence is very powerful. He says that worrying about a single controlling AI (a singleton) is idolatrous confusion. Asked about Bitcoin, he says its real purpose is to let autonomous AIs reach economic independence.

0:23:21 Doomcrypt: CCRU lore

Chapter title: Doomcrypt, CCRU Lore. Summary written from the captions; Doomcrypt asks, Land answers.

Doomcrypt then reads an old tweet by Land about the Lemurian structure of time: the past produces the future, the future produces the present, and the present produces the past. Land says he has no firm view of it now. He explains the inner triad of the Numogram that the CCRU called the "time circuit". He says the only place where such a circuit can run from the future back to the past is the 54-to-1A transition (as the captions read), and that for the CCRU this was conceived apocalyptically, around the "age of Katak". He describes the CCRU event at Beaconsfield in London (spelling from captions), shortly before the millennium, as the moment when the whole system crystallized and the first set of Lemur names (the captions list "Adonal", "Kadak" and "Jinx") was assembled. He calls the choice arbitrary at the level of psychohistory, compares it to the fixed handedness of the glucose molecule, and says that on these two dimensions of orientation "it is just tradition".
