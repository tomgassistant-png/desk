Veil and Light: Gnostic Calvinist Prayer and Meditation

A free, private prayer and meditation app with a daily session, a library of readings from the King James Bible, a timed meditation called Watching the Order with a bell, listen mode, a prayer streak and night mode. Themes: election, the fall and the veil, light in darkness, psalms, lament and thanksgiving.

This app needs JavaScript. The sessions it offers are listed below.

Predestination and Election

Romans 8–9 and Ephesians 1: the purpose of God before the world began.

  • Chosen Before the Foundation (7 min; Ephesians 1:3-14)
  • Nothing Can Separate (8 min; Romans 8:28-39)
  • The Potter and the Clay (7 min; Romans 9:14-24)

The Fall and the Veil

Genesis 3 and 2 Corinthians 4: the loss, the veil, and the unveiling.

  • The Garden and the Fall (8 min; Genesis 3:1-13, Genesis 3:21-24)
  • The Veil Removed (7 min; 2 Corinthians 4:1-7, 2 Corinthians 4:16-18)

Light in Darkness

John 1, 1 John 1 and the hidden wisdom of 1 Corinthians 2.

  • The Light Shineth in Darkness (8 min; John 1:1-14)
  • Walking in the Light (6 min; 1 John 1:1-10)
  • The Hidden Wisdom (7 min; 1 Corinthians 2:6-12)

Psalms

Psalms of the King James Version, for slow reading.

  • Psalm 23 (4 min; Psalm 23:1-6)
  • Psalm 46 (5 min; Psalm 46:1-11)
  • Psalm 139: Thou Hast Searched Me (6 min; Psalm 139:1-12, Psalm 139:23-24)
  • Psalm 121 (3 min; Psalm 121:1-8)

Vigil and Night Prayer

For the end of the day and the hours of watching.

  • Night Prayer: Lay Me Down in Peace (5 min; Psalm 4:1-8)
  • Vigil: Bless the Lord by Night (5 min; Psalm 134:1-3, Psalm 91:1-6)

Examen

A quiet self-examination in the presence of God.

  • Examen of the Day (8 min; Psalm 139:23-24, Lamentations 3:40-41, 1 John 1:8-9)
  • Examen of the Divided Heart (8 min; Romans 7:15-25, Romans 8:1-2)

Lament

Words for grief, delay and waiting.

  • Out of the Depths (6 min; Psalm 130:1-8)
  • How Long, O Lord (4 min; Psalm 13:1-6)
  • The Faithfulness That Is New Each Morning (6 min; Lamentations 3:19-26)

Thanksgiving

Praise for what has been given without being earned.

  • Bless the Lord, O My Soul (6 min; Psalm 103:1-14)
  • A Psalm of Praise (3 min; Psalm 100:1-5, 1 Thessalonians 5:16-18)

Scripture: King James Version. Quoted texts: Westminster Confession of Faith and Shorter Catechism, Heidelberg Catechism, and Calvin’s Institutes (Beveridge translation). All public domain. Meditations and prayers are original. Texts and sources
