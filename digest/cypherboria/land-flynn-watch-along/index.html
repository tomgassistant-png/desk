Glossary: Land's vocabulary in plain English

AccelerationismThe idea that the way through capitalism and technology is to speed them up rather than resist them. "Left" accelerationists, like the hosts, want the speed-up to serve people; Land's version treats capital and AI as forces that will outgrow humans.
HyperstitionCCRU's word for fictions that make themselves real: an idea or story that spreads and, by spreading, helps bring about what it describes.
The OutsideLand's term for whatever lies beyond human experience and control: the future, the alien, the inhuman. In this episode it shows up as answers coming "from the outside."
The CathedralA term Land took from the blogger Curtis Yarvin (Mencius Moldbug) for the network of universities, media and civil service that he says sets acceptable opinion. Not used in this episode, but it is central to his politics.
CCRUThe Cybernetic Culture Research Unit, an informal group around Land and Sadie Plant at the University of Warwick in the mid to late 1990s.
NumogramCCRU's number diagram linking the digits 0 to 9, used as a system of divination. Carlson held one up on air in 2025.
Lemurs and NeolemurianismCCRU's name for the entities linked to the Numogram, borrowed from William Burroughs's story "The Ghost Lemurs of Madagascar." Neolemurianism was CCRU's mock-religion around them. Lemures were Roman spirits of the dead.
Gnostic CalvinismLand's recent label for his theology: the Calvinist belief in God's total control over history, read together with the Gnostic belief that this world is a kind of prison or simulation.
Empty summitLand's image for a system with no one at the top, like markets (the invisible hand) or neural networks, that he says can direct things more powerfully than a single ruler.
Gematria and the AQ cipherTurning letters into numbers and looking for meaning in the totals. "AQ" (Alphanumeric Qabbala) is a cipher associated with CCRU.
SingletonAn AI-safety term for one AI or authority that ends up controlling everything.
Supralapsarian / infralapsarianTwo Calvinist views on whether God decided who is saved before or after deciding to allow the Fall.

Claims to check

  • Flynn says Geoffrey Hinton is Charles Howard Hinton's great-grandson. That matches published family histories, but Flynn also called Hinton Ilya Sutskever's mentor "at OpenAI"; Hinton supervised Sutskever at the University of Toronto and did not work at OpenAI.
  • Flynn says Marc Andreessen's "book" names Land. The best-known mention is Andreessen's 2023 essay "The Techno-Optimist Manifesto," which lists Land among its "patron saints." It is an essay, not a book.
  • The claim that Musk's grandfather Joshua Haldeman was in the Technocracy movement and was arrested in Canada is widely reported; the 1930s "tweet" robot card was shown on screen but not independently checked here.
  • Flynn's claim that Robert Graves wrote a chapter of William Sargant's Battle for the Mind (1957) is unconfirmed.
  • Flynn's claim that Robert Plant was reading The White Goddess when he wrote "Stairway to Heaven" is unconfirmed.
  • The claim that a Wild Wild West episode inspired Anton LaVey's look is Flynn's and is described by him as alleged. Unconfirmed.
  • Flynn says Constance Cumbey died the summer before his Tucker interview. Unconfirmed.
  • Flynn's account of a Tucker Carlson producer asking for a Land interview, and a planned Shanghai trip in November, comes only from Flynn on this recording. Unconfirmed.
  • Black Sabbath's first album came out on Vertigo, a Philips label, and the Philips founders were related to Karl Marx through his mother's family. Flynn calls the founder Marx's "first cousin"; the exact relationship should be checked.
