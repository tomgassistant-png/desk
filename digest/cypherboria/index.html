NewLand joins the online "Jesuit Cybernetic Culture Research Unit" (JCCRU) panel and answers its Christian critique of the CCRU

The JCCRU is a panel organized by Ryan Haecker of the Methexis Institute, a research institute based in Austin, Texas. It was part of the 51st Patristic, Medieval and Renaissance (PMR) Conference at Villanova University, held 9 to 11 October 2026. The panel met online and streamed live on YouTube on Friday, 9 October, from 10:15 a.m. U.S. Eastern time (5:15 p.m. Israel time). Its stated aim is a Jesuit, Trinitarian critique of the original CCRU of the 1990s. Land joined remotely from Shanghai, late in the session after technical trouble with a new computer. He spoke about a return of theological debates, including the debate between Reformed and Roman Catholic Christianity, in a culture shaped by cybernetics and AI. He said the Bible cannot be read literally, said his own access to the tradition is "neo-Pythagorean" and numerical, and described his intellectual path since the CCRU as "retrochronic" rediscovery rather than a change of views. Other speakers included Aaron Khokhar, Darius Branche, Ashton Arnoldy and John Esposito. The full stream and an edited 13-minute clip of Land's response are on YouTube; Haecker announced Land's participation on X on 9 October.
