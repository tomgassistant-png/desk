Trenton

Daily research digest section · latest edition 16 October 2026

Background

Trenton, the capital of New Jersey, had an estimated 91,506 residents in July 2025, slightly more than the 90,871 counted in the 2020 census, and up from 84,913 in 2010. In the Census Bureau's 2020–2024 survey, the median household income was $52,537, about 21.6 percent of residents lived in poverty, 37.9 percent of homes were occupied by their owners, and 16.2 percent of adults held a bachelor's degree. The median value of an owner-occupied home was $160,700 in that survey; the city reports that the average sale price in 2026 so far is about $239,000. The city's current strategy rests on state and federal money for specific projects: the Choice Neighborhoods program in the North Ward, the repurchase of the empty downtown hotel, the sale of vacant city-owned houses, and grants to bring small businesses into the downtown.

16 October 2026 · today

NewCouncil votes to buy back the empty downtown Marriott with $4 million in state money

On 6 October the City Council authorized the city to seek a $4 million grant from the New Jersey Economic Development Authority to buy the former Lafayette Yard Marriott at 1 West Lafayette Street. The purchase depends on receiving the grant, so the city's own money is not at risk. The city does not intend to run the hotel. It plans to hand the building to Tetrabilt, the redeveloper the Council has already chosen, to reopen it as a modern hotel for state government, businesses and conventions. The building has been closed for years; the vote does not complete the purchase.

16 October 2026 edition · City of Trenton · NJ.com · Central Jersey Media

NewState awards up to $54.3 million for 107 affordable senior apartments in the North Ward

The New Jersey Economic Development Authority approved an Aspire tax credit of up to $54.3 million, or 85 percent of the cost, for THA Senior, a four-story building planned for 800 Martin Luther King Jr. Boulevard. It will have 107 one-bedroom apartments for residents aged 62 and older, together with space for early childhood education, job training and senior services. The project also has a $900,000 federal Choice Neighborhoods grant and low-income housing tax credits. It is part of the city and Trenton Housing Authority plan to rebuild the North Ward. The award was announced on 17 September.

NewMeridian Furniture buys 10 acres on Enterprise Avenue for a warehouse expected to bring about 200 jobs

On 15 September the City Council approved the sale of more than 10 acres of city land at 300 to 366 Enterprise Avenue to Meridian Furniture for $1 million. The company, founded in Trenton more than 20 years ago, plans a warehouse of about 220,000 square feet. The city expects about 200 jobs. The figure of 200 jobs comes from the city; the land sale and the size of the building are confirmed by public planning records.

16 October 2026 edition · City of Trenton · Quadratín Hispano · Trenton planning agenda

NewCity offers 28 abandoned homes and buildings for sale, with priority for buyers who will live in them

From 1 to 30 October the city is taking online applications for 28 city-owned properties in all four wards. Many need major repairs, and the city has published photographs of each one. Priority goes to buyers who will repair the property and live in it. The mayor said the goal is to get each building fixed, occupied and back on the tax rolls.

16 October 2026 edition · City of Trenton release, 30 Sep · New Jersey Globe

NewCity says average home sale price has risen 5.8 percent this year

According to the city, Multiple Listing Service data show the average sale price of a Trenton home rose from about $226,000 in 2025 to about $239,000 so far in 2026. Unconfirmed: this is the city's own reading of the data, and the outlets that reported it repeated the city's figures. Realtor.com and Redfin show recent median sale prices near $250,000, which is consistent with a rising market but is not the same measure.

NewTrenton's new professional hockey team, the Ironhawks, plays its first home game on 24 October

The Trenton Ironhawks, a new ECHL team affiliated with the New York Islanders, open their first season at CURE Insurance Arena on Saturday, 24 October, at 6:00 PM against the Adirondack Thunder. The team will play 36 home games in the city, which brings visitors downtown on game nights.
