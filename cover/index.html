Israel election

NewSohlberg turns down Likud's request for probe details before results are approved Wednesday: "Elections have results"

Otzma Yehudit says it will vote against approving the results in the committee (Ynet; Maariv). Gadi Eisenkot called Likud's move "a holding battle" and a direct attack on democracy, and Yair Lapid called it an attempt to cancel Israeli democracy (Ynet; Maariv). A Kan headline says the results will go to the President even without the committee's approval; the article itself was not opened. On the investigation itself: Ynet reports the Shin Bet is racing to finish by Wednesday a probe of money transfers to a political figure, on suspicion of election bribery, amid tension between Shin Bet chief David Zini and the agency's legal adviser. JNS, citing Channel 14, says Zini complained of obstruction by legal advisers during a six-hour meeting with Sohlberg, and Haaretz reports Zini had sought to delay the results until Sunday. A Kan headline says the evidence of Qatari involvement is thin (headline only). Srugim reports the Attorney General now requires her own approval before Joint List aides are questioned; Likud calls this selective enforcement. Separately, a police complaint was filed over a threat against Eisenkot's wife, Hana (Times of Israel feed headline; Kan and Israel Hayom headlines).

Brazil

NewFlávio Bolsonaro appeals the seizure of his passports and asks that Moraes be declared barred from the case

The defense argues that Moraes cannot judge a case involving Fraction 024, the company that owned a jet Moraes used, because of ties between the company and the law firm of his wife, Viviane Barci de Moraes; that Banco Santander told the court Flávio was not a partner, holder or user of the Alcazar companies' accounts; and that the flight-risk finding rested on acts of others, such as Eduardo Bolsonaro's statements (R7). It wants the case moved to Justice André Mendonça, who oversees the INSS pension-fraud inquiry (G1). Flávio left Brazil in defiance of the order and arrived in Miami on 28 October.

France presidential election

NewAttal says a primary of the center and right is "inevitable" despite Philippe's refusal; a giant "XXL" poll is floated as an alternative
