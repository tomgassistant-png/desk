Israel election

NewElections committee approves the official results 15–2 and hands them to Herzog: Likud 27, Yashar 21, B'Yachad 12

Final seats: Likud 27, Yashar 21, B'Yachad 12, Joint List 11, Democrats 8, Ra'am 8, Yisrael Beytenu 7, Otzma Yehudit 7, United Torah Judaism 7, Shas 6 and Religious Zionism 6. By the reports' bloc count that is 53 for the current coalition parties, 48 for the opposition parties and 19 for Arab parties (Arutz Sheva; Al Jazeera). According to JNS, 5,717,397 people voted, and records from 9,069 polling stations, about 75% of regular stations, were reviewed after the fraud claims; the committee rejected those claims (Al Jazeera). A turnout figure of 77.9% appears only in a government search result that could not be opened, so it is unconfirmed. In the committee's presidium, the Likud and Shas members abstained and Sohlberg cast the deciding vote; Likud MK Amit Halevy resigned from the committee in protest, and Itamar Ben-Gvir said "The elections were stolen" (Arutz Sheva; JNS). Herzog said the suspicions of foreign interference must be investigated, but that "an entire community must not be smeared" (JNS). The new Knesset is sworn in on 10 November. The person Herzog chooses gets 28 days to form a government, with a possible 14-day extension (Arutz Sheva).

Lebanon, Syria and the Druze

NewYnet: Hezbollah's civilian bodies return to Zawtar al-Gharbiyeh, a village handed to the Lebanese army as a pilot

According to the Alma research center, quoted by Ynet, Hezbollah-linked civilian bodies, the Islamic Health Organization and "Wa Ta'awanu," are active again in Zawtar al-Gharbiyeh; Alma documented the same in Frun, the second pilot village, in October. The IDF struck two suspects on a motorcycle in Qarbiha, killing one, Nasser Mohammed Younes, whom Ynet describes as a medic with Amal's Risala Scouts. Ynet says talks on the monitoring mechanism have been stuck for months. No new Israeli reaction to the EU training mission (EUSM) and no new contributor countries were found today.

Brazil

NewCentral bank holds the Selic at 13.75%, ending five cuts, citing a "significant increase in uncertainty" after Lula's reelection

The rate had been 15% from June 2025 to March 2026. This was the first meeting since Lula's reelection, which unsettled markets, and the statement cites a "significant increase in uncertainty." The Copom raised its own inflation projections: 5.6% for 2026 (from 5.2%), 4.7% for 2027 (from 3.9%) and 3.5% for the second quarter of 2028 (from 3.1%). The market's Focus survey expects 5.2% for 2026 and 4.8% for 2027. The board is led by central bank governor Gabriel Galípolo (InfoMoney; Estadão).

France presidential election

NewNational Assembly rejects the revenue half of the 2027 budget 458 to 0, killing the bill in first reading; Le Pen gains on Polymarket

The prime minister's office pushed hopes of a compromise to "December." Budget minister David Amiel said the amendments the deputies had voted would have raised the deficit to 5.9% of GDP, against a 5% target (5.4% is expected this year; the plan sought about €43 billion in savings). The government could still use article 49.3 or pass the budget by ordinance (France 24). A Senate start date of 20 November comes only from a search snippet and is unconfirmed. No new poll was published since the Ifop/LCI poll reported here on 4 November. Polymarket (winner of the next French presidential election) at about 4:23 AM Israel time on 5 November: Le Pen 41.45% (up 1.3 points in 24 hours), Lisnard 15.85% (up 0.5), Mélenchon 13.5% (up 1.0), Philippe 11.5%, Lagarde 6.75% (down 1.1), Glucksmann 2.65%, Hollande 2.45% and Bardella 2.25%.

SpaceX, xAI and Cursor

NewSpaceX picks Qualcomm for Starlink Mobile's US ground-based 5G network: "tens of thousands" of sites from 2027

Qualcomm says it is its largest 5G infrastructure deal to date. It will supply QRU100 and QDU100 platforms and the lowest software layer; SpaceX builds the equipment and the rest of the software. Deployment starts in 2027 and will cover "tens of thousands" of large cell sites, using massive MIMO antennas in the 2 GHz band (Fierce Network). CNBC notes that Starlink made 36% of SpaceX's revenue and 85% of its operating profit in the third quarter, and that SpaceX last month agreed to buy 800 MHz spectrum and is reported to be in talks over Boost Mobile.
