Israel election

NewHerzog picks Eisenkot to form the next government; mandate ceremony today at 5 PM; Netanyahu now calls for a "unity government"

Eisenkot, recommended by 67 Knesset members against 46 for Netanyahu, will have 28 days, with a possible extension of up to 14 days; if he fails, Herzog has three days to decide whether to try a second candidate (Jerusalem Post; Ynetnews). Shortly before the announcement, Netanyahu used the word "unity" for the first time since the election, calling for a national unity government of "all the parties of the national camp and all the Zionist parties" while keeping his allies, and warning that a government relying on Arab parties would "dismantle the State of Israel from within" (Ynetnews; JNS; Jerusalem Post). Yashar! called it a "hysterical speech" and said Likud could join only if it broke with "the extremists," accepted Eisenkot's four principles and served under him (JNS; Ynetnews). The coalition math is still unsettled: Avigdor Liberman rejects a minority government supported by Arab parties and is negotiating with the Haredi parties on his own; Shas called reports that it would join an Eisenkot government "fake news" after an N12 report that it would not block a coalition backed by United Torah Judaism (Ynetnews; Jerusalem Post). Arutz 7 reports that the change bloc and the Arab parties have filed more than 61 signatures to replace Knesset Speaker Amir Ohana when the new Knesset is sworn in on Tuesday. Update: see the separate election interference allegations note below for the broader evidence and responses watch; a filed appeal has not been verified. The Central Elections Committee's official final results page is unchanged (turnout 77.98%; Likud 27, Yashar! 21, B'Yachad 12). Polymarket now gives Eisenkot about 66% and Netanyahu about 18% to be the next prime minister.

France presidential election

NewMélenchon presents his program at Le Bourget before more than 20,000 people and refuses to soften it; the Communist Party says its deputies who back him have left the party

He named about thirty headline measures, including a price freeze, a higher minimum wage, wages indexed to inflation, a Sixth Republic and non-alignment abroad, and for the first time set out a position on artificial intelligence (more national control without rejecting it); he said he is sure he can beat Marine Le Pen (franceinfo). The same day the national council of the Communist Party (PCF) said any member who backs a candidate other than its leader Fabien Roussel puts himself "outside the party"; the statement targets deputies Stéphane Peu, Elsa Faucillon and Soumya Bourouaha, who announced on Friday that they support Mélenchon (franceinfo). Le Monde reports that Le Pen and Édouard Philippe attacked the LFI program before the rally, and that Saturday's social protests drew 146,000 people by the Interior Ministry's count and 250,000 by the youth union coalition's count, ahead of Monday's social conference at the prime minister's office (Le Monde headlines; articles blocked to automated readers). No new national poll was published. Polymarket: Le Pen about 45.0% (45.2% yesterday), Lisnard 14.9%, Mélenchon 13.5% (up from about 12.5%), Philippe 9.5%.

Brazil

NewFlávio Bolsonaro tells the Washington Post that the electoral court chief "does not know what he is talking about" and that Trump no longer sees Brazil as a full democracy

Flávio, who lost the 25 October runoff to Lula by 4.4 million votes, said Donald Trump "is convinced that Brazil is no longer a full democracy," that he now needs to "regularize" his immigration status in the United States, and that he will "never" say how he left Brazil (Folha, citing the Post). The Post cited an unnamed Brazilian security official who said police are investigating whether a network of American, Argentine and Israeli agents helped him leave; that claim is single-source. Nunes Marques was appointed to the Supreme Court by Jair Bolsonaro. Estadão, O Globo and Gazeta do Povo carried the same quotes in their headlines.

Quebec

NewSt-Pierre Plamondon: "not one more cent" for Nemaska Lithium's Bécancour plant; Legault asks whether the PQ wants Quebec's lithium in foreign hands

The Journal de Montréal's investigative bureau had reported that the plant could run short of money to finish construction. Former premier François Legault, who launched the project, answered under the post that Nemaska owns the Whabouchi lithium mine and asked whether the PQ wants all of Quebec's lithium mines owned by foreigners and all processing done abroad (La Presse Canadienne).

Colombia and Israel

NewIsrael's ambassador in Bogotá: an Israeli defense industry delegation came this month, and Israel's Defense Ministry director general will lead a larger one at the end of November

Aisen, in Colombia for two months, listed the steps taken since President Abelardo de la Espriella restored relations broken off by Gustavo Petro: the mission was raised from consulate to embassy, Colombia's ambassador has opened her embassy in Jerusalem, the ban on coal exports to Israel was lifted, visas between the two countries were dropped, and the free trade agreement, which Petro's government had tried to cancel, was reactivated by an exchange of notes. She said 2027 will mark 70 years of relations, with an Israeli film festival planned, and chose Medellín as her first trip outside the capital to seek farm and business ties. The delegation dates come only from this interview and are not confirmed elsewhere.

SpaceX, xAI and Cursor

NewSpaceX sets Starship Flight 15 for 13 November, pending approval, with the first attempt to catch both the booster and the ship

Super Heavy may try to return to the Starbase tower for a catch six to eight minutes after liftoff, and SpaceX plans to bring the Starship upper stage back to the launch site for a catch about 22 hours after launch, both only if strict conditions are met; if both work it would be the first full recovery of an orbital launch system, and nearby Cameron County residents may hear sonic booms (KRGV). Elon Musk called the double catch "the critical breakthrough needed to make life multiplanetary" (X @elonmusk), and SpaceX describes the mission as the first orbital Starship launch carrying advanced Starlink satellites (X @SpaceX). The earlier FAA planning date was 8 November. No new entries appeared in the Grok Bot changelog or the Cursor changelog since yesterday.

Non-Latin script digitization

NewIndia's Culture Ministry plans an AI platform to transcribe, translate and search more than 10 million manuscripts under Gyan Bharatam

Officials say a technology firm is being hired; the system is meant to transcribe, translate, identify authors, reconstruct faded text and link manuscripts across collections, with every AI answer backed by page-level citations. More than 1.25 crore manuscripts in many languages have been reported on the Gyan Bharatam portal, of which 11.53 lakh (1.153 million) are published, and the program has 491.66 crore rupees sanctioned for 2025 to 2031 (New Indian Express). These figures come from one report and are not independently confirmed here.
