Israel election

NewElection chief Sohlberg: the vote was "conducted properly" and the foreign-funding probe will not change the results; Likud and Ben-Gvir object

Likud called the statement "very puzzling" and pointed to the 4,612-vote margin, saying "a black cloud" hangs over the result. Itamar Ben-Gvir said there was "improper pressure" on the Shin Bet chief and called it a cover-up. The Joint List's chairman answered that "the mountain gave birth to a mouse" and that "67 seats said 'go home'" (Maariv). Separately, a court on Sunday ordered the release of two United Arab List (Ra'am) activists arrested on Election Day on suspicion of buying votes, finding the evidence had weakened, and extended a third suspect's detention by two days (Haaretz). There are still no new official results: the Central Elections Committee is due to give the official results to President Herzog on Wednesday, 4 November. Its results site could not be reached (it returned "not found" at every address tried).
