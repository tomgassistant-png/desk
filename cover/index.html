Israel election

NewEisenkot takes the mandate and calls for a "national emergency government"; Deri leaves the Shas Knesset list; Knesset sworn in Tuesday

Shas chairman Aryeh Deri left the party's Knesset list along with Moshe Abutbul and Dror Amos, and remains party chair; Uriel Busso and Yossi Taieb enter the Knesset in their place (ynet; Hezki Baruch, Arutz 7, on X). Channel 13's Yoeli Brim reported a secret deal between Avigdor Liberman and United Torah Judaism: Housing (for Yaakov Asher) and Interior for UTJ, and an immediate halt to arrests of yeshiva students who do not report for the draft. That is a single-outlet report. Liberman called it "all lies and fabrications" (Srugim, citing Channel 13). Asked about Eisenkot on CBS's 60 Minutes, Donald Trump said he and Netanyahu are "very similar" (ynet). Itamar Eitam was named faction chair for Religious Zionism (Hezki Baruch on X). The 26th Knesset is sworn in on Tuesday, 10 November ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=31.7765,35.2054)). Arab48, citing Maariv, reports a letter from a forum of 168 former security officials warning that up to 1,500 right-wing activists, some possibly armed, may try to disrupt the ceremony, and that police and the Shin Bet have prepared a special plan. That warning comes from an interested group and has not been confirmed by police. The Central Elections Committee's official results page is unchanged: turnout 77.98%; Likud 27 seats, Yashar! 21, B'Yachad 12, Joint List 11, Democrats 8, Ra'am 8, Yisrael Beytenu 7, Otzma Yehudit 7, UTJ 7, Shas 6, Religious Zionism 6.

Lebanon, Syria and the Druze

NewFinland prepares to join the EU security mission in Lebanon; FakeReporter counts a sharp rise in online attacks on Druze after the election

From Sweida, Suwayda 24 reported people extorting families of detained National Guard members.

Israeli data centers

NewBezeq and Adgar plan a 450 million shekel underground data center in Petah Tikva

Each side is to invest 200 to 250 million shekels and share revenue equally; Adgar provides the land, building and a firm 16-megawatt grid connection, and Bezeq buys equipment and runs operations. The parties have 90 days of exclusivity to sign a detailed deal. Bezeq expects 50 to 80 million shekels a year in revenue once the site fills (Globes). The site is planned to Tier 3 standard, with a term of 24 years and 11 months (People & Computers).
