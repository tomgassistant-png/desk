Israel election

NewHerzog: Eisenkot got 67 recommendations, Netanyahu 46; mandate decision on Sunday

Herzog said he will hold personal talks with some party leaders over the Israeli weekend before deciding. Eisenkot's recommendations came from Yashar!, B'Yachad (Together), the Democrats, Yisrael Beytenu, Ra'am and the Joint List; Netanyahu's from Likud, Religious Zionism, Otzma Yehudit, Shas and Zehut; United Torah Judaism recommended no one (Ynetnews; Hindustan Times / PTI). Yashar! said Eisenkot's 67 was the highest recommendation count since 2015 and called for the mandate without delay (Israel National News). The Central Elections Committee's official final results page now lists turnout at 77.98% (5,717,397 voters of 7,331,981 eligible), confirming the figure that was marked unconfirmed in yesterday's edition (votes26.bechirot.gov.il).

France presidential election

NewElabe: Macron's trust falls to a record 13%, Lecornu to 17%; Le Pen still first at 35%, and Polymarket moves up

Marine Le Pen remains first in personal trust at 35% (down 2), Jordan Bardella second at 33% (down 4), and Marion Maréchal third at 27%. Christine Lagarde, after leaving the ECB, rises to 27% and leads Macron's own voters jointly with Édouard Philippe; Gabriel Attal falls to 22%. After Wednesday's unanimous rejection of the 2027 budget's revenue half, coverage focuses on article 49.3 or, after the 70-day parliamentary deadline on 17 December, a budget ordinance under article 47 (20 Minutes). Polymarket (winner of the next French presidential election) at about 4:20 AM Israel time on 6 November: Le Pen about 44.25% (up from 41.45% in yesterday's digest), Lisnard about 15.25%, Mélenchon about 12.5%, Philippe about 11.5%, Hollande about 2.35%, Glucksmann about 2.75% and Bardella about 2.25%.

Brazil

NewAfter the Selic hold, Lula convenes his economic team; Durigan says fiscal measures wait until he returns on 15 November

Durigan said the Thursday meeting presented "scenarios and proposals," that the diagnosis was "quite convergent," and that options include reviewing spending, benefits and budget controls, while avoiding cuts to social programs Lula has pledged to keep (G1). He also said current interest rates are "very high and very harmful" and that the government must create conditions for them to fall in a "sustainable and structural" way (Reuters via WTVB). Brazil's gross debt is reported at 82.9% of GDP after a rise of more than 10 percentage points in Lula's current term (same Reuters copy). No package was announced on Thursday.

SpaceX, xAI and Cursor

NewGrok Bot can now place phone calls, the official @bot account says

The demo, as described by grokbot.guru from the official post, shows a Phone call card with Cancel and Call before any dial, a live transcript during the call, and Take over and End call controls; the sample call turned down a retention discount after the user set that limit in advance. The post did not say which plans or countries get the feature, or what it costs. The public Grok Bot changelog at x.ai still listed 0.78.1 (27 October) as latest when checked this morning, so the phone feature is confirmed from the X account rather than from a new changelog entry. Terms section 2.7 still applies to calls, according to the secondary write-up.
