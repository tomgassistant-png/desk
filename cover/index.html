Lebanon

NewEU sets up a training mission for Lebanon's army and police as UNIFIL prepares to leave

The mission has a military part, which will advise and train the Lebanese Armed Forces on border and maritime security, and a civilian part, which will train the Internal Security Forces on cybercrime and anti-smuggling. The EU says it is not a replacement for UNIFIL, whose mandate ends at the end of 2026. Euronews reports that it will not operate in southern Lebanon. An EU official said personnel could deploy in the coming months. Rapporteur reports, citing confidential plans, that the mission will have about 100 military personnel, mostly in Beirut, with France and Italy competing to lead it; this has not been confirmed by a second source. Asharq Al-Awsat writes that the aim is to free the Lebanese army to concentrate on disarming Hezbollah.

SpaceX, xAI and Cursor

NewGrok Bot 0.70.2: Bots get their own email addresses and can search X

Version 0.70.2, released on 11 October, lets a Bot claim its own email address and receive mail there. Any email it sends from that address waits for the user's approval. Bots can now also search X and read posts without a connected X account, and some connectors, such as Figma, Notion and Canva, can appear as interactive cards in the chat. The release also lets Bots pay with X Money or Stripe Link. The official changelog is the source. The Botski Blog, an unofficial site, also reports the X search and the email feature, but it does not confirm the version number or the payment feature, which are therefore unconfirmed by a second source.
