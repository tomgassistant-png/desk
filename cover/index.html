Israel election

NewNetanyahu calls for a unity government "without preconditions"; Eisenkot calls it a bluff, rejects rotation, and the two teams meet on Sunday; Kahana's speaker vote is set for Tuesday

Eisenkot rejects a rotation of the prime minister's post and will not negotiate with Itamar Ben Gvir's Otzma Yehudit; Arab48, citing Israeli reports, says he will offer Netanyahu a unity government without rotation. ynet reported that people around Netanyahu say rotation could be discussed, and that a Likud minister says Netanyahu would insist on serving first, in part to be positioned for the presidency in May 2028 (unnamed sources). ynet's analysis describes three tracks Eisenkot is running at once: with the Haredi parties, with the Arab parties on Knesset votes, and with Likud, and calls Sunday's meeting "mainly symbolic." Finance Minister Bezalel Smotrich joined the call for unity, and Likud MK Elisha Madan set up a "unity tent" outside the Knesset, joined by MK Itamar Itam, according to Arutz Sheva reporter Hezki Baruch on X. The Knesset is to elect Matan Kahana (Yashar!) as speaker on Tuesday, 17 November, replacing Amir Ohana, and Avigdor Liberman is to replace Boaz Bismuth as chair of the Foreign Affairs and Defense Committee, ynet reported. No change to the official results and no Likud appeal filing was found.
