Israel election

NewOfficial results go to Herzog at noon today and consultations start at 5:00 PM; the Shin Bet chief asks to question four Arab party leaders, and the Attorney General pushes back

Shin Bet chief David Zini has asked to question four senior figures in the Arab parties under caution, among them MKs Mansour Abbas, Sami Abu Shehadeh and Ahmad Tibi (Ynet; Kan; The Times of Israel says this was without providing evidence). Attorney General Gali Baharav-Miara pushed back, and committee chairman Noam Sohlberg said the committee has no authority over investigations and that the results will be published as planned (Ynet; Haaretz headline). The Joint List said Zini had "gone off the rails" (Maariv and Haaretz headlines). According to The Times of Israel, 16 coalition members of the committee asked for information and seek to block the official publication of results (feed headline and summary); Ynet reports that Likud will abstain and Otzma Yehudit will vote against. In the final count, 968 votes for Arab parties were struck out, with no change in seats; Likud claims a gap of 4,612 votes (Arutz Sheva). Separately, the High Court ruled that Knesset Speaker Amir Ohana violated its earlier ruling and "harmed the order of government," and ordered him to pay 10,000 shekels in costs (Kan; Makor Rishon headline).

France presidential election

NewIfop poll for LCI: Le Pen at 35 to 37 percent and Mélenchon at 17.5 to 18 percent in every first-round scenario

Among the center and right, Édouard Philippe does best: 15 percent if he faces only Bruno Retailleau (7 percent) or only David Lisnard (9 percent). With Gabriel Attal as the center's candidate against Retailleau and Lisnard, Attal gets 9 percent, against 5 and 7.5. With all four in the race, they fall to 9.5 (Philippe), 7 (Lisnard), 5 (Attal) and 4 (Retailleau). Raphaël Glucksmann stays between 10 and 13 percent. Ifop's Frédéric Dabi said Mélenchon's lead over the others is beyond the margin of error. Le Parisien and Le Figaro reported the same poll (headlines only). Polymarket at about 4:30 AM Israel time on 4 November: Le Pen 40.15 percent (40.25 a day earlier), Lisnard 15.35 (15.75), Mélenchon 12.5 (11.5), Philippe 11.5 (unchanged), Lagarde 7.85 (8.3) and Hollande 2.2.

Israeli data centers

NewEnlight expects the freeze on connecting large power users to end on 7 December and says its Ashalim data-center project is "in a very good place in the line"
