Israel election

NewZman Yisrael poll: Yashar 26, Likud 20; blocs still 54–49

A Zman Yisrael poll for the Times of Israel, fielded on 14–15 October (500 respondents, margin of error 4.4 points), gives Gadi Eisenkot's Yashar 26 seats and Benjamin Netanyahu's Likud 20. The anti-Netanyahu Jewish-led bloc has 54 seats and the Netanyahu bloc 49, both short of 61, with Arab parties at 13 and the nonaligned Reservists at 4. Ofer Winter's Amcha Yisrael is at 2.8%, below the 3.25% threshold. Last week's Zman Yisrael split was 55–51 the other way on the blocs; the party gap between Yashar and Likud has widened from 24–20 to 26–20 (Times of Israel).

Lebanon

NewEUSM Lebanon: Italy, France and Spain expected to supply most of about 100 troops

Reporting on the force plan for the EU Support Mission in Lebanon (established by the Council on 12 October) says Italy, France and Spain are expected to provide most of a permanent military component of about 100 personnel, based mainly in Beirut, with no planned deployment to southern Lebanon near the Israeli border. France is pushing to lead the mission at first; Italy has already named a command candidate; command is expected to rotate yearly (Rapporteur, 9 October; details confirmed against the Council decision of 12 October). The mission advises and trains the Lebanese Armed Forces and Internal Security Forces on border, maritime and related tasks; it is not a UNIFIL replacement. Brazil

NewDatafolha: Flávio 47%, Lula 45% in the runoff, still a technical tie

Datafolha's runoff poll for Folha de S.Paulo, published on Thursday (2,520 interviews on 13–14 October, margin of error 2 points, TSE registration BR-00466/2026), has Flávio Bolsonaro at 47% and Lula at 45%. Among valid votes the split is 51% to 49%. A week earlier Datafolha had Flávio at 49% and Lula at 45%; the gap has narrowed. The poll is the first Datafolha after large pro-Lula street events. Rejection is 46% for Lula and 44% for Flávio (G1; Folha).

Quebec

NewAll 59 PQ MNAs sworn in; cabinet still due 21 October

On Thursday 15 October, all 59 Parti Québécois members of Quebec's National Assembly were sworn in at the Red Room in Quebec City, taking an oath of allegiance to the people of Quebec rather than to the King. Premier-designate Paul St-Pierre Plamondon spoke of the party's largest caucus since 1998 and said cabinet, of about 20 ministers, is still due on 21 October. Liberal MNAs are to be sworn in on Monday, Québec solidaire on Tuesday and Conservatives on Thursday. A court challenge to the 2022 law that dropped the oath to the King remains pending (Canadian Press via CityNews; Radio-Canada).

Colombia and Israel

NewColombia withdraws from the Hague Group after opening its Jerusalem embassy

Foreign Minister Omar Bula Escobar announced Colombia's withdrawal as a member and co-chair of the Hague Group, a coalition that sought legal and diplomatic pressure on Israel over Gaza. Colombia's Foreign Ministry said on 15 October that Colombia had formally left; Bula said the group was "created to isolate and attack Israel" and that Colombia rejects that approach. Bolivia and Honduras had already left. He spoke after the 14 October embassy inauguration in Jerusalem, with Netanyahu and Gideon Sa'ar present, condemned the October 7 massacre, and criticized the prior Colombian government's record on terrorism and antisemitism. The embassy opening itself was covered on 15 October; the Hague Group exit is the new diplomatic step (Colombian Foreign Ministry; Ynet).

SpaceX, xAI and Cursor

NewSpaceXSI releases Grok Voice 3.0; tops Artificial Analysis speech-to-speech ranking

On 14 October SpaceXSI released Grok Voice 3.0, its newest speech-to-speech model for the Voice Agent API. Independent benchmark site Artificial Analysis ranks it first on the Speech-to-Speech Index at 86.9, ahead of OpenAI's GPT-Live-1 (Astra, medium) at 83.2 and Google's Gemini 3.8 Live (Extended Thinking, High) at 82.6; Grok Voice 2.0 scored 81.3. SpaceXSI and Artificial Analysis both report about 97% on Full Duplex Bench. API price is unchanged at $0.08 per minute ($4.80 per hour). The alias grok-voice-latest now points to grok-voice-3.0. SpaceXSI says the model powers millions of Tesla in-car conversations a day and about 20,000 Starlink support calls a day (company figures). SiliconANGLE separately reported the launch and the Artificial Analysis ranking (SpaceXSI; SiliconANGLE).
