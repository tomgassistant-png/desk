Israel election

NewEisenkot and Liberman agree the next Knesset speaker will come from Yashar!, reportedly Eisenkot himself; Eisenkot drops a fast minority government; the Knesset is sworn in today

After meeting, the two said they are fully confident they can form a stable government for four years (Hezki Baruch, Arutz 7, on X). United Torah Judaism turned down a request from Yashar!'s faction chair-designate Roy Folkman that it and Shas join a move to replace Speaker Amir Ohana at the Knesset's opening session (Hezki Baruch on X). Srugim, citing Maariv's Anna Breskin, reported that Eisenkot does not plan a fast swearing-in of a minority government backed by the Arab parties because people in his bloc say it has no real chance and too little support even inside the bloc; signals of progress in talks with the Haredi parties were not confirmed by Haredi sources. ynet, citing Channel 12, reported that Netanyahu's close associate Natan Eshel repeatedly asked Ra'am chair Mansour Abbas, before and after the election, whether Ra'am would help Eisenkot in the Knesset; Ra'am did not answer. Likud said it did not know of the exchanges and that they were not approved by the prime minister. Eshel first denied the contacts, then said he had only asked "some Arabs" whether they had "a drop of self-respect". The same day Netanyahu released a video again calling for a "unity government" led by him with the Haredi parties. The 26th Knesset's 120 members are sworn in today, Tuesday, 10 November ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=31.7765,35.2054)), with no government yet (ynet). The Central Elections Committee's official results page was unchanged when checked on 10 November (turnout 77.98%).

France presidential election

NewJudo champion Teddy Riner declares for president; Toluna Harris Interactive for RTL and M6: Le Pen 34–36%, Mélenchon 18–19%, Philippe slips again

In a video he said France needs "common sense, firmness, justice" rather than "a professional politician", and in an interview with Le Parisien he said he plans to keep competing, even at the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics "if the office allows" (franceinfo). The ninth wave of the Toluna Harris Interactive poll for RTL and M6, taken online 3 to 5 November among 2,101 registered voters (before the 7 November cost-of-living protests, and before Riner's entry), puts Marine Le Pen at 34% to 36% depending on the scenario, up 1 to 2 points, and Jean-Luc Mélenchon at 18% to 19%. Édouard Philippe falls to 14% as the only center candidate (down 2) and 11% if Gabriel Attal also runs, level with Raphaël Glucksmann; David Lisnard reaches 9% in some scenarios and Bruno Retailleau is at 7% to 10%. 66% favor a broad primary of the center and right, and 48% expect Le Pen to win (RTL). On Polymarket, checked about 4 AM Israel time on 10 November, Le Pen is at 45%, Lisnard 14%, Mélenchon 13.5%, Philippe 9.5% (up from about 7.5% on 9 November) and Riner 2.4%.

Brazil

NewMeta tells the electoral court it did not favor Flávio Bolsonaro's side; it says Lula's posts reached about 1.5 times as many people

Meta says Lula's posts reached about 1.5 times as many people, and drew about 1.5 times as many interactions within 24 hours, as Flávio Bolsonaro's (organic reach, not ads); Flávio posted less often but reached more people per post; and government and opposition accounts reached similar audiences from 16 to 25 October, the runoff date. It says most candidates' reach came from their own followers, as TSE rules require, and that deputy Nikolas Ferreira's very high view counts reflect people rewatching his videos. In late October the court's inspector general, Ricardo Cueva, at the request of Lula's coalition, ordered Meta not to boost posts from two Flávio Bolsonaro accounts and one Nikolas Ferreira account through the end of the runoff, on pain of a R$500,000 fine. This is the company's own account; the court has not ruled on it.

SpaceX, xAI and Cursor

NewSpaceX seeks a tax break for a $24 billion gas power plant and "super-compute facility" at Starbase, Texas; public hearing on 9 December

The city commission will consider a property tax abatement under Chapter 312 of the Texas Tax Code and a related economic development agreement under Chapter 380 of the Local Government Code at 7 p.m. on Wednesday, 9 December. Nothing has been approved. The notice gives no other detail. The 956 Report, a Rio Grande Valley news site, says the notice was posted on 6 November and was first reported by Gateway to Mars, and that a pending state air-permit filing for SpaceX's "Boca Chica Facility" lists 35 temporary gas turbines of about 748 megawatts in a first phase, later to be replaced by eight larger turbines of about 708 megawatts; that filing was not checked here. Neither the notice nor SpaceX says what the computing would be used for. Reuters and Bloomberg reported the plan on 9 November.

UK breakup politics

NewThe National: a freedom-of-information reply shows the Cabinet Office has polled on Scottish independence under Burnham without publishing results; the SNP wants them released

The SNP is demanding the release of what it calls "secret independence documents". The paper sets this against Burnham's statement at Prime Minister's Questions in September that there is no majority for another referendum and that the conditions should be "exactly the same" as for Irish unity. Former SNP MP Tommy Sheppard said the government should release the details and that not doing so would be "corruption". This is one outlet's report; the FOI reply itself was not published with the article, and no UK government response was found.
