Israel election

NewFinal count: Likud 27, Yashar! 21; the Netanyahu bloc ends at 53 seats, the Eisenkot bloc at 48 and the Arab parties at 19

Compared with the count at about 90 percent, Likud and Ra'am each gained a seat and the Joint List and Shas each lost one. Turnout was 77.9 percent: 5,716,665 votes were counted, 5,690,778 of them valid, and the 3.25 percent threshold came to 184,947 votes (Maariv; Israel National News; Ynet). Committee chairman Justice Noam Sohlberg is to present the official results to President Herzog on 4 November. The committee's legal adviser, with Sohlberg's agreement, said the Shin Bet's investigation into foreign interference does not affect the results. A Ynet analysis says the Netanyahu bloc fell from 64 seats in 2022 to 53 even though it won about 83,000 more votes; its share of the vote fell from 48.38 percent to 42.1 percent. Likud won about 1,163,400 votes (20.51 percent), and Shas fell from 8.25 percent to 5.26 percent. The timeline from here, per Ynet: Herzog begins consultations with the parties on 4 November, the new Knesset is sworn in on 10 November, Herzog is to assign the task of forming a government by 11 November, and the candidate then has 28 days, which can be extended by 14. Ynet counts 47 new members among the 120.
