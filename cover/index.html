Israel election

NewChannel 12 poll: Eisenkot's bloc 54, Netanyahu's 49; Winter again below the threshold

A Channel 12 Midgam poll published on Monday evening (509 respondents, margin of error 4.4 points) again put Gadi Eisenkot's bloc at 54 seats and Benjamin Netanyahu's at 49. Yashar leads with 23 seats, Likud has 20, Bennett's Beyachad (Together) 13, the Democrats and Yisrael Beytenu 9 each, the Joint List, Otzma Yehudit and Shas 8 each, United Torah Judaism 7, Religious Zionism 6, Ra'am 5 and the Reservists 4. Ofer Winter's Amcha Yisrael again falls short of the 3.25 percent threshold, at the equivalent of about 1.8 seats. Parties opposed to Netanyahu would hold 67 seats if the Arab lists are counted, but parts of Eisenkot's bloc refuse to sit with them, so the Zionist opposition alone remains short of 61 (Haaretz).

Lebanon

NewKatz: if Hezbollah joins an Iranian attack, Israel will take more land around Nabatieh and the Litani

Defense Minister Israel Katz said on Wednesday, during a visit to the IDF buffer zone in southern Lebanon, that if Hezbollah joins Iran in an attack on Israel, it will lose more territory around Nabatieh and the Litani River as part of a permanent Israeli security zone, and that the IDF is prepared for an expanded invasion. He also said another evacuation of the Dahiyeh district of Beirut would follow, and that more than one million Shiites would be displaced. The Jerusalem Post notes that this is the first specific Nabatieh threat from Katz in several months, and that many of his election-season threats have not led to major new operations. Israel already holds a strip of southern Lebanon up to about 10 kilometers from the border (Jerusalem Post; This Is Beirut).

Brazil

NewQuaest's first runoff poll: Flávio 46%, Lula 44%, a technical tie

Quaest's first national runoff poll, published on Wednesday for Globo and O Globo (2,154 interviews, 10–13 October, margin of error 2 points, TSE registration BR-08729/2026), has Flávio Bolsonaro at 46% and Lula at 44%, a technical tie. One percent are undecided and 9% say blank, null or will not vote. Rejection is almost equal: 54% say they would never vote for Flávio and 53% for Lula. Ninety-one percent say their choice is final. Compared with Quaest's pre-first-round runoff scenario, both candidates rose by two points as blank and null fell. On Polymarket Flávio is at about 84% and Lula at about 16%, down slightly for Flávio from about 86% on 12 October. The runoff is on 25 October; the Band debate is on 18 October and Globo's on 23 October (G1; Exame; Polymarket).

Colombia and Israel

NewColombia opens its embassy in Jerusalem; Sa'ar and Bula preside

Colombia inaugurated its embassy in Jerusalem on Wednesday 14 October, becoming the tenth country with an embassy in the city. Israeli Foreign Minister Gideon Sa'ar and Colombian Foreign Minister Omar Bula Escobar unveiled the sign; Sa'ar affixed a mezuzah. Sa'ar said that when he took office there were five embassies in Jerusalem and that President Abelardo de la Espriella had kept a campaign promise to move Colombia's mission there. Bula said relations "should never have been broken" and called Israel a strategic partner. Ambassador Tania Gilinski will head the mission. The free-trade agreement, cancelled under Gustavo Petro, was restored the day before. The Palestinian Authority and Jordan condemned the move (Israel National News; Al Jazeera / AFP).

SpaceX, xAI and Cursor

NewCursor opens an Origin App Marketplace with more than 20 partner apps

On 13 October Cursor's changelog announced an Origin App Marketplace with more than 20 apps for deployments, CI, code review, security, monitoring and project management. Named partners include Cloudflare, Netlify, Render, Spacelift and Vercel for deployments; Buildkite, CircleCI and others for CI; CodeRabbit, Greptile and Tenki for code review; Artemis Security, Semgrep, Socket Security and Upwind for security; Sentry for monitoring; and Linear for project management. Public submissions are open: teams build against the Origin API, submit a slug, tags and a demo video, and Cursor reviews before listing. There is nothing new on the official Grok Bot changelog since version 0.70.2 (11 October), already reported, and nothing material on SpaceX beyond the Flight 15 catch claim already covered on 12 October (Cursor changelog).

Quebec

NewParti Québécois MNAs to be sworn in today; cabinet due 21 October

The 59 newly elected Parti Québécois members of Quebec's National Assembly are to be sworn in on Thursday 15 October; Liberal opposition members on 19 October; and the PQ cabinet on 21 October (Radio-Canada; CBC). Premier-designate Paul St-Pierre Plamondon says he will not swear an oath of allegiance to the King, relying on Quebec's 2022 law that made the National Assembly oath the only required oath. That law is under court challenge; an injunction hearing is expected between 19 October and 9 November. St-Pierre Plamondon has asked government lawyers whether he can keep his campaign pledges to bar the lieutenant-governor from the opening speech and to redirect the lieutenant-governor's office budget (about 1.3 million Canadian dollars for 2026–27) to homelessness work. Governor General Louise Arbour has said she is skeptical of those plans (CBC; Radio-Canada).
