France presidential election

NewThird Saturday of protests: 320,000 marchers by official count, fewer than last week; 520 arrests and a curfew in parts of Paris

There were clashes at the end of the Paris march at Porte de Charenton, in Marseille (an undeclared march of up to 10,000), in Lyon (Place Jean-Macé) and in Rennes (13,500 marchers). Arrests reached 511 by 8 PM by franceinfo's count and 520 by France 24's, against about 1,000 a week earlier; police made 40,000 checks. Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez blamed "ultra-left" groups and called a Molotov cocktail thrown at a gendarme in Paris an "attempted murder" (the gendarme was lightly hurt). A new curfew covers some parts of Paris from 11 PM to 6 AM on the nights of Saturday and Sunday (France 24). Next: high-school and university blockades on Tuesday and Thursday, a strike and march day called by the CFDT, CGT, FO, Unsa and FSU on Thursday, 5 November, and a yellow-vest (gilets jaunes) call to continue on 6–7 November. Quebec

NewCarney offers to meet Premier St-Pierre Plamondon within about ten days and lists federal money for Quebec

Carney listed federal commitments to Quebec: the Port of Montreal's Contrecœur terminal, $10 billion over ten years for infrastructure, $10 billion toward the Churchill Falls and Gull Island projects, $11 billion for icebreakers from Davie, and $4.7 billion for VIA Rail cars from Alstom's La Pocatière plant. He wants to hear the premier's intentions on the Churchill Falls deal with Newfoundland and Labrador, and plans for the 2028 Francophonie Summit in Ottawa-Gatineau to be organized with St-Pierre Plamondon and New Brunswick Premier Susan Holt. The federal budget is due on 5 November, and Carney said any trade deal with the US would still leave some tariffs in place.

SpaceX, xAI and Cursor

NewFrom 7 November, xAI will put an invisible watermark in Grok API text, to meet the EU AI Act

The mark covers ordinary text, structured output and tool-call arguments on all endpoints. It works by shaping which words the model picks, adding no hidden characters, and a response header, x-watermark, reads "true" when output is marked. Team administrators can turn it off per model in the console by attesting that a legal exception applies, but some models cannot be opted out. Detection is statistical and available on application (xAI docs; TeslaNorth).
