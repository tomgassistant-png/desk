Israel election

NewIsrael votes today: 7.33 million eligible voters, 12,018 polling stations and 36 lists; polls open 7 a.m. to 10 p.m.

Ynet reports 7,331,981 eligible voters, 12,018 polling stations and 36 party lists. Polls are open from 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.; in small towns, hospitals, prisons and nursing homes they close at 8:00 p.m. There are about 250 polling stations in hospitals, and special stations for residents still displaced from Be'eri ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=31.4242,34.4911)), Kfar Aza ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=31.4833,34.5333)), Nir Oz ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=31.3096,34.4008)) and Holit ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=31.2319,34.3265)). Lebanon, Syria and the Druze

NewSweida (Syria): Jordanian air strikes after the Karak church attack hit Shahba; Syria says 7 dead and closes the Nassib crossing

The 23 October attack on a Greek Orthodox funeral in the village of Hamoud killed 9 people and wounded at least 34; three suspects, two brothers and a brother-in-law, were arrested after a shootout in al-Judayyda (Al Jazeera; Reuters via Al-Monitor). Syrian Civil Defense said four sites were hit in Daraa and Sweida provinces and seven people were killed, including three children and a woman; one strike hit a residential building in Shahba, in Sweida province, killing a 60-year-old man, and five members of one family died in a tent in Al-Tai, Daraa (Al Jazeera). The Times of Israel's live blog headline later put the toll at 8. Syria denies Jordan's claim that the strikes were coordinated with Damascus and closed the Nassib crossing, Jordan's only open crossing with Syria (Al Jazeera; Al-Monitor).

Brazil

NewBolsonaro's party accepts Lula's win; Moraes bars Flávio Bolsonaro from leaving Brazil; Trump calls the vote extension "very strange"

PL president Valdemar Costa Neto said in a note: "Reconhecemos o resultado presidencial, proclamado pelo TSE" ("We recognize the presidential result proclaimed by the electoral court"), while still questioning social spending before the vote. The final count gave Lula 64.2 million votes (51.7 percent) and Flávio 59.8 million (48.2 percent) (Agência Brasil). Moraes barred Flávio from leaving the country and ordered him to surrender his passports in the inquiry involving lawyer Willer Tomaz and Alcazar Holdings (Madeira); the decision cites a remark by his brother Eduardo that Flávio should leave the country if he lost (Agência Brasil). Trump said: "I like Lula… a very bad look… The extension given was very strange" (Jerusalem Post); Ynet quotes him calling it "another rigged election". Justice Cristiano Zanin had extended voting by two hours at the Workers' Party's request; both campaigns had asked for an extension.

Marc Andreessen

NewAndreessen publishes a new essay on AI agents and the "self-driving company"

On 26 October 2026, Marc Andreessen published an essay titled "Let Chaos Reign, and Then Rein In Chaos" (subtitle "Replit and the dawn of the self-driving company") on the a16z newsletter site, and posted it as an X article from @pmarca at 16:08 UTC (18:08 Israel time). It was written for the launch of Replit for Business, a product of the a16z portfolio company Replit. He argues that important workplace technologies such as the personal computer, the spreadsheet and email first arrived in a disorderly way, and that companies then had to build controlled settings that kept employee initiative alive. He says AI agents are following the same path, and that companies will become "self-driving" when people set goals and agents carry out the work. It is his first written essay found since "Investing in Cognition" on 9 September 2026, and his first original X post found since 18 October. His X posting is still far below his pre-July level: his total post count rose by 4 in the day to 27 October, and he made 47 posts in the seven days to 27 October (about 7 a day), compared with about 25 a day before July.
