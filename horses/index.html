American Prairiebaseline

Land conservation (bison; BLM grazing permits) · MT

Bozeman, Montana nonprofit building a prairie reserve in northeast Montana with a herd of about 1,000 bison. It does not run a wild horse program. It matters here because BLM revoked six of its bison grazing permits on May 8, 2026, in a dispute over how BLM treats grazing permits on public land. A federal judge paused part of that on Sept 4.
