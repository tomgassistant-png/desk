Amishav Melet's open letter to Ambassador Huckabee, and David Friedman's reply (September 2026)

Saved October 26, 2026 (Israel time, UTC+2) at Tom's request. Two posts, each copied in full, word for word from X, with nothing changed: Melet's open letter of September 5, then former U.S. Ambassador David Friedman's open letter to Huckabee of September 6, which follows it below. Related: Ambassador Huckabee and "settler violence": statements and visits.

Who he is and what he is replying to

Amishav Melet (@amishav_ on X) is a farmer from the Shiloh Valley and a member of a local community security patrol. On Saturday, September 5, 2026, Ambassador Mike Huckabee visited the Palestinian-American town of Turmus Ayya and posted a video he had filmed of an ATV (a security "Ranger") passing near the house where he was meeting residents, calling the riders "a band of trespassers." Melet wrote this post that night, after the Sabbath ended, to say he was the driver. His brother Benayahu Melet, 32, was shot dead on July 24, 2026, near the village of Tell; he signs the letter as "a bereaved brother."

Full text

Dear Mike Huckabee,

Now that Shabbat has ended, I saw your post and the alerts across the news channels about your visit today to Turmus Ayya — and about those “dangerous and threatening settlers” whom you apparently saw driving a security Ranger.

*Well, that was me. I was the driver of the Ranger you filmed.*

And I want you to hear directly from me what actually happened today.

This morning, our shepherd, who was out with the flock, reported that dozens of vehicles had gathered at the edge of the village — precisely in an area from which, over the past several months, there have been repeated attacks and arson incidents against us.

I got into our security Ranger to check what was happening, and at the same time we notified the IDF as per protocol.

When we arrived and saw respectable people and media crews, we said hello and continued on our way. Shortly afterward, the army informed us that this was a proper, coordinated visit.

That is the entire story.

And yet, within minutes, we were portrayed in the media as “dangerous settlers.”

Amabssador Huckabee, I will respectfully relate some facts to you that they surely didn’t share with you today.

For example:

1. Until recently, the head of the Turmus Ayya municipality was *Lafi Shalabi*, who was a partner in assisting in the murder of the youth Yehuda Guetta, of blessed memory. While he was in prison, the village chose him to head the municipality — as a sign of honor for his actions.

2. A member of the Turmus Ayya municipal council, *Abu Samra*, with whom you were photographed today in warm handshakes, is a released terrorist, who is photographed with weapons and published incitement to murder and attacks; in his view, Jews as well as Americans are the enemy. He also led the opposition in the village to the hanging of American flags on the houses, and following the struggle, the flags were removed from the Turmus Ayya municipal building. Only in recent years, Abu Samra was personally involved in several cases of attacks and looting of Jews and their property in the Shiloh area settlements.

3. Throughout the village there are dozens of monuments glorifying terrorists and terrorism, alongside a map of the State of Israel painted in the colors of Palestine, which testifies to their true intentions.

4. *Abla al-Kuk*, the manager of the girls’ high school who was photographed with you today, has for years been involved in educating generations of students with messages of incitement, encouragement and glorification of terrorism. She also personally participated in and led violent riots against settlers and security forces.

5. In the past year alone, hundreds of incidents of attacks have occurred, including stone throwing, arson, lynchings and shooting, by Arabs from the village against shepherds, farmers, innocent Jewish travelers who had just returned home, as well as against army forces.

6. You also met with the wife of the “martyr” *Omar al-Katin*, who was killed after attempting to harm police officers, and with additional representatives of families of those who attempted to harm soldiers and innocent civilians.

7. We also saw how the ambulance drivers in Turmus Ayya showed you videos that were edited and cut in a biased manner. In the incident in question, the ambulance deliberately blocked an army jeep while hundreds of Arabs pelted it with stones. When the soldiers had no choice and were forced to get out of the area in order to protect their lives, they lightly struck the edge of the ambulance in order to extricate themselves. They also did not tell you that the entire incident was an attempt to run toward the Israeli communities. You also saw videos presented to you today by ambulance drivers from the village. I ask you to watch the full footage, rather than edited clips.

When you cut out the beginning of an incident, you can make almost any story look completely different.

I am a bereaved brother.

Reply: David Friedman's open letter to Ambassador Huckabee, 6 September 2026

David Friedman (@DavidM_Friedman on X) was the U.S. ambassador to Israel from 2017 to 2021 and is the founder of Channel 18 Israel. On the morning of September 6 he reposted Melet's letter (at 10:54 Israel time), then wrote this open letter to Huckabee. He later met Melet on September 10, and on September 18 he said the embassy's Palestinian Affairs Unit "withheld some key facts" in arranging the Turmus Ayya visit.

Original post: https://x.com/DavidM_Friedman/status/2096513989731578206
Posted: September 6, 2026, 11:21 Israel time (08:21 UTC). Views: 145,075 (as of October 26, 2026, with 1,507 likes, 355 reposts, 57 quotes, 203 replies).

Full text

To my dear friend Mike Huckabee,

No one doubts where your heart is. Your support for Israel is unwavering and historic.

There is violence in Judea and Samaria — there has been for many years. But Jews don’t wake up in the morning saying “I’m going to go kill a Palestinian today.” The opposite, of course, is not true — hundreds and hundreds of Jewish residents of J&S have been murdered by Palestinian terrorists.

Most Palestinian residents of J&S cheered 9/11, 10/7, support Hamas and want to push the Jews out of all of Israel, not just J&S.

So of course there is violence between Jews and Arabs in Judea and Samaria. But the two questions are 1) why does it exist, and 2) how is it being used by Israel’s enemies?

It exists for the reasons described above — Jews need to defend themselves. Some acts of defense are debatable, some may be inappropriate. But they are acts of defense nonetheless against ruthless thugs that simply refuse to live in peace on any terms with their Jewish neighbors.

The “settler violence” libel is being used, as you recognize, as part of a coordinated PR effort by the same people who falsely accuse Israel of genocide. The Palestinian-Americans who had no difficulty building beautiful houses in the area are now draping American flags on their houses so that attacks against them — which they instigate — can be filmed as attacks against America.

It’s all a cynical campaign and its roots go back more than a century.

Of course I agree that settler violence hurts Israel’s “standing” in the world, but it hurts less than giving Palestinian terrorists free rein to kill more Jews.

Unfortunately, labeling the Jews who fight back as “terrorists” is a win for the anti-Israel PR machine who then create a moral equivalency between both sides. Your correct observation that the Jews committing acts of violence are a tiny fraction of the total Jewish population is a distinction entirely lost on the media, who have a false narrative to uphold.

A note on the claims (Melet's letter)

Everything in the letter is Melet's own account. The shepherd's report, his call to the army and the army's reply rest on him alone, and so do his seven points about the town's officials and residents (the former mayor, the council member, the school principal and the others). Other sources have not confirmed them. That is a note about sourcing, not a judgment of him: he is a named eyewitness and the driver of the vehicle in question, and his brother's death is confirmed by Ynet, the Jerusalem Post and Arutz 7. Huckabee's own account of the visit, and the Palestinian side, are in the Huckabee backgrounder.
